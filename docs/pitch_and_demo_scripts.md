# Pitch and demo scripts

The full, word-for-word scripts and slide content live in the shared doc
"DemandRange: Final Scripts and Slide Content". This file keeps the essentials
so the repo stays self-explanatory.

**Required deliverables:** demo video (3 to 5 minutes, link), pitch deck (up to 5
slides, PDF link), one-page summary (PDF), disclosure statement.
**Optional:** pitch video, build files. Submit at hackathon.anneru.com/submit by
Sun Nov 1, 12:00 PM ET.

**One sentence:** DemandRange tells a mini-grid developer, before building, how
much power a village will use and what system it needs, with a range a lender
can check.

**Coach's four questions, in one line each**
1. Problem: nobody can show a lender a defensible site-level demand forecast before building.
2. Who benefits: developers, lenders and funders, and villagers.
3. Value: size right before a field survey; a range lenders can check; a fairer tariff.
4. Unique: open, coordinate-based range and growth, turned into system size and the cost of being wrong.

**Slides (5):** 1 Title and team. 2 Problem and unit economics. 3 Solution and value.
4 Cost, affordability and sustainability. 5 Evidence, scale, ask and team.

**Rules for every video and slide:** say "simulated" or "real" with every result;
every number has a source; show it on screen instead of claiming it.
